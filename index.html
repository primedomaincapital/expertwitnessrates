The Thesis

Expert fees are negotiated in the dark. This domain turns on the light.

Ask a litigator what a forensic accountant charges per hour and you will get an anecdote. Ask what a neuroradiologist bills for deposition testimony and you will get a range so wide it is useless. Expert witness rates live in a fog of word-of-mouth, gated directories, and retainer agreements nobody shares. The result is friction everywhere: attorneys overpay, experts underprice, and fee disputes burn billable hours before a case is even filed.

That fog has real legal consequences. Daubert challenges probe whether an expert's fee arrangements compromise independence. Courts scrutinize billing records. And when an expert's rate is questioned on the stand, the absence of any public benchmark turns a straightforward question — "Is this rate customary?" — into a credibility problem.

The market is enormous and growing: $5B+ in annual U.S. expert witness spend, spanning thousands of specialties from structural engineering to digital forensics. Yet no single publication owns rate transparency across specialties. The directories that exist charge experts for placement and tell attorneys nothing about what a specialty actually costs.

ExpertWitnessRates.com is the natural home for that authority — a specialty-by-specialty rate benchmark, a referral engine for vetted experts, or the data layer under a litigation-analytics product. The name states exactly what it is. In a market built on credibility, the address is the argument.
